## What Creates the Quality

The film does not rely on complex 3D. Its quality comes from controlled evidence:

1. A short branded hook establishes the series immediately.
2. Real logo and real product screens arrive before decorative explanation.
3. The film alternates between a calm floating proof card and an immersive full-frame UI crop.
4. Fast motion appears only at section boundaries and settles quickly.
5. The central body is a dense run of real demonstrations joined by hard cuts.
6. Numbers, price/access facts, and language support are shown on official pages and annotated in place.
7. Captions describe exactly what the viewer can currently see.
8. The final frame remains a useful product state rather than becoming a generic CTA card.

## Generalization Boundary

Generalize the viewer job, not the literal PhET content:

- “Credibility proof” may be usage volume, customers, awards, reviews, published case studies, or another official trust signal.
- “Friction proof” may be free access, no login, open source, browser-based use, export availability, or another verified objection remover.
- “Access path” may be language selection, install, upload, search, account creation, export, or another real first-use path.
- The number of demonstrations may vary from three to five.

Do not generalize beyond the measured four layout modes or motion recipe set without a new approved calibration.
